### Assistente (resposta final) · 08:28

- **P1 — Persistence can turn an actual refusal into unavailability.** `src/infrastructure/quote/trace.py:50` awaits recording before returning the HTTP outcome, inside Hedge/Retry’s timing. Reproduced with virtual time: refusal after 10 ms + recorder taking 4 s → two HTTP calls, `QuoteUnavailable`, total 7.5 s. SQLite’s 5-second busy timeout makes this realistic. Bound or decouple persistence so it cannot delay the outcome past the quote budget.

- **P2 — Incorrect normalization flag around New Year.** `src/infrastructure/quote/trace.py:63` uses UTC `created.year` for cancelled calls, while HTTP normalization uses `clock.today().year`. During São Paulo’s final three hours of December 31, the recorded flag can contradict the submitted payload.

No further concrete privacy or correlation-isolation defects found. Tests cover immediate recorder errors but lack slow recording and concurrent logical calls.

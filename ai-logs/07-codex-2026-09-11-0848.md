One important finding:

- **P1 — Shutdown can hang indefinitely** in [recorder.py](<local>/Entrevista/namastex-test-tecnico/src/infrastructure/tracing/recorder.py:39). When shutdown cancels both `_drain` and `finish`, `_drain` resolves only its current delivery future and abandons queued events. `finish` suppresses cancellation while awaiting the last queued future, which never resolves. Reproduced with two queued events and `asyncio.run` shutdown; process timed out (exit 124). Handle worker cancellation so every pending barrier settles, and test simultaneous worker/caller cancellation. Existing coverage cancels only `finish`.
